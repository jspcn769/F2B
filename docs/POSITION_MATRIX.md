# F2B Position Matrix

## Position codes
- **BA — Base:** primary organizer, ball-handler and playmaker; controls tempo, creates advantages and directs the offense.
- **ES — Escolta (Shooting Guard):** perimeter scoring, vertical threat, isolation, off-ball finishing and shot creation.
- **AL — Aler (Small Forward):** versatile wing; combines scoring, athleticism, transition, creation and physical versatility.
- **AP — Ala-pivot (Power Forward):** physical two-way forward; rebounding, defense, interior work, connective play and off-ball scoring.
- **P — Pivot (Center):** interior presence, rim protection/intimidation, rebounding, screening and close-range finishing.

## Real-height guide
Height is measured before applying the +15 cm F2B conversion.

| Real height | Primary plausible positions | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Below 1.74 m | BA or ES | AL is exceptional and needs a strong role/physical case. |
| 1.74–1.82 m | BA, ES, AL | AP is possible only with an unusually strong physical/role rationale. |
| 1.82–1.88 m | AL, AP, P | BA is exceptional, not an automatic exclusion. |
| 1.88–1.93 m | AL, AP, P | Role and athletic profile determine the best fit. |
| Above 1.93 m | P; AP in selected cases | AL/AP require a clear mobility and role rationale. |

## Assignment priority
1. Real height.
2. Football role and functional responsibilities.
3. Versatility and physical profile.
4. Coach/system context, when supported.
5. Roster coherence.

Do not force every team to have a conventional basketball positional balance. Duplicated positions and missing positions are allowed.

## Initial archetype families
The following are starting families, not a complete final taxonomy:
- BA: pure creator/deep organizer; offensive scorer; intense ball-winner/creator; defensive disruptor; physical organizer.
- ES: explosive dribbler/vertical attacker; creative offensive guard; pure scorer/killer; intense attacking guard.
- AL: elegant creator; explosive dribbler; complete scorer; powerful offensive wing; slasher/penetrator.
- AP: complete all-rounder; defensive physical enforcer; box-to-box forward; versatile organizer.
- P: scoring finisher; interior reference; mobile versatile big.

The project intends a more detailed archetype catalogue (including 15 BA archetypes). Do not invent the missing full list from these family labels; recover and validate it from the original approved design discussion before treating it as canonical.

## Pilot-specific approvals
- Lionel Messi (Barcelona 2008/09): **ES — APPROVED** by explicit user decision.
- Xavi Hernández: BA appears as a prior draft proposal only; status remains **DRAFT**, not approved.
- Other pilot player positions: unassigned pending review unless a later explicit decision is recorded.

## Rotation and roster conventions
- Five players on court.
- 48-minute game assumption: 240 total player-minutes per team per game.
- Typical roster range: 10–16 players, not a hard limit.
- Position duplication and positional gaps are permitted.
