# Project Overview

## 1. Purpose
F2B reimagines association football history as professional 5v5 basketball. It is not a simple statistical reskin: it is a structured alternate sporting universe whose historical foundations remain traceable to real football.

## 2. What remains historically anchored
- Club identity and historical continuity.
- Player identity and real career chronology.
- Real club-season associations, subject to first-team eligibility.
- Competition identity, naming, historical seasons and agreed format.
- Source-backed biographical facts.

## 3. What can diverge
Within the F2B universe, basketball roles and performance, player ratings, team strength, match outcomes, league tables, champions, awards, records, transfers/contracts and salaries may differ from football history. Such divergence must be labelled ALT or SIM and must never be presented as real-world history.

## 4. Player eligibility
Only football midfielders and forwards are eligible, including all relevant sub-positions. Goalkeepers and defenders are excluded. Eligibility is assessed from the player's actual football role and season context, not from a later basketball conversion.

## 5. Physical conversion
Apply the global conversion consistently:
- Height: real verified height + 15 cm.
- Weight: real verified weight + 7 kg.
Position assignment uses real height before conversion. Converted measurements must never overwrite the real measurements.

## 6. Historical coverage and roster scope
The initial database period starts in 1974/75. A season roster represents eligible players who genuinely belonged to the first team in that season. Do not add reserve-team players solely because they made a cameo appearance.

## 7. Competition model
Competitions retain their agreed football-derived identity, naming, season chronology and format, while being played as basketball. Competition-specific rules and any deliberate format changes must be documented rather than assumed.

## 8. Intended knowledge graph
The long-term database should connect:
- Players and biographies.
- Clubs and club identities.
- Seasons and season rosters.
- Player-club spells and transfers.
- Competitions, editions, standings and matches.
- Basketball statistics, ratings and awards.
- Contracts, salaries and transactions.
- Honours, records and historical milestones.
- Sources, evidence, decisions and simulation events.

## 9. Non-negotiable data discipline
Every record must distinguish fact from conversion and simulation. Unknown values stay unknown; estimates are labelled; conflicting sources are recorded; approvals are explicit. No mass generation is considered canonical without review.
